# Internal Linking Map

*Rule: Create dense, semantic relationships to establish the Topical Authority Model.*

## 1. Authority Hub Pages (`/authority-approvals/[slug]`)
**Outbound Internal Links To:**
- Relevant **Project Types** (e.g., DDA links to Commercial Fit-Out, Warehouse).
- Relevant **Services** (e.g., DCD links to Fire Safety Design).
- Authority-specific **Guides/FAQs** (e.g., "How to register on DDA portal").
- Real **Projects/Case Studies** executed under this authority.

## 2. Project Type Pages (`/project-approvals/[slug]`)
**Outbound Internal Links To:**
- The regulating **Authorities** (e.g., Warehouse links to DM, Trakhees, DCD).
- The required **Engineering Services** (e.g., Warehouse Mezzanine links to Structural Engineering, MEP).
- Project-specific **Guides**.
- Real **Case Studies** proving Goldland's execution of this exact project type.

## 3. Service/Engineering Pages (`/services/[slug]`)
**Outbound Internal Links To:**
- **Authorities** that mandate this engineering discipline.
- **Projects** that utilize this service heavily.
- **Team Members** (Engineers) certified in this service.

## 4. Case Studies & Projects (`/projects/[slug]`)
**Outbound Internal Links To:**
- The parent **Project Type**.
- The parent **Authority**.
- The applied **Services**.

## 5. Guides & FAQs (`/guides/[slug]`)
**Outbound Internal Links To:**
- **Primary CTA:** Must link directly to the commercial Authority or Project Approval page.
- **Contextual:** Links to specific Services mentioned in the text.

## Linking Rules
- Avoid exact-match anchor text spam (e.g., linking "Dubai Municipality" 15 times on one page).
- Anchor text must flow naturally within the sentence.
- Utilize dedicated "Related Content" or "Required Engineering" card grids at the bottom of templates to automate these semantic links cleanly without cluttering body text.
