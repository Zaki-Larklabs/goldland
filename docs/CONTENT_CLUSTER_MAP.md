# Content Cluster Map

*Rule: Prioritize first-hand expertise, real evidence, and actual Goldland knowledge. DO NOT generate generic AI filler.*

## Cluster 1: Authority Approvals
**Pillar:** `/authority-approvals`
**Sub-pillars:** DM, DDA, DCD, Trakhees, DEWA, RTA, JAFZA, DIEZ, Dubai South, DHA, DHCC, Concordia.
**Evidence Requirements:** 
- Actual procedural steps based on Goldland's engineering experience.
- Verified timelines (e.g., "Typically 5-7 working days for DCD").
- Real NOCs/Certificates (redacted) in the Evidence Gallery component.

## Cluster 2: Project Approvals
**Pillar:** `/project-approvals`
**Sub-pillars:** Warehouse, Mezzanine, Villa Modification, Commercial Fit-Out, Restaurant, Clinic, Office, Pool.
**Evidence Requirements:**
- Before/After interactive sliders.
- Real case studies linked directly.
- Specific engineering bottlenecks and how Goldland solves them.

## Cluster 3: Design & Engineering
**Pillar:** `/services`
**Sub-pillars:** Architectural, Structural, MEP, HVAC, Fire Safety, Shop Drawings.
**Evidence Requirements:**
- Team member profiles showcasing certifications (e.g., "DM Certified Structural Engineer").
- Embedded technical diagrams or snippets of Goldland shop drawings.

## Cluster 4: The Proof Hub (Case Studies & Evidence)
**Pillar:** `/projects`
**Content:** Deep-dive case studies.
**Structure:** 
1. The Challenge (e.g., Client needed DDA and DCD approval in 14 days).
2. The Engineering Solution (e.g., Expedited MEP drawings, direct coordination).
3. The Result (e.g., NOC achieved in 12 days).
4. Real Client Review component.

## Cluster 5: Knowledge Hub
**Pillar:** `/guides`
**Content:** Granular, long-tail FAQs and procedural breakdowns.
**Source Material:** Must be derived from Goldland's internal engineers and real client questions, NOT scraped from generic contractor blogs.
