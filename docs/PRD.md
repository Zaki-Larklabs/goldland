# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
The new Goldland Contracting LLC digital platform is designed to replace the legacy static website with a full-stack, SEO-driven architecture. 
It focuses on Authority Approvals, Design & Engineering, and Project Management for Dubai fitout projects.

## 2. Target Audience
- Businesses, property owners, and project stakeholders needing fitout approvals in Dubai.
- Needs include: Mezzanine approvals, warehouse fitouts, commercial office setups, and villa modifications.

## 3. Core Features
- **Dynamic Content & SEO**: Programmatic generation of authority and project approval pages.
- **Project Database**: CMS-backed records of completed projects (case studies, before/after evidence).
- **AI Assistant**: A chatbot fine-tuned on Goldland's proprietary and verified approval knowledge.
- **Lead Generation**: Multi-step qualification forms.

## 4. Source of Truth
Content is governed by a strict hierarchy prioritizing Goldland's verified operational knowledge over competitor information. Unverified claims (e.g., "100% approval rate") are prohibited.
